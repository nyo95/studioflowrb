## Owner decisions (2026-10-24)

1. The office has only Designers and Drafters (and the Platform Owner). There is no manager today.
2. **Manager** is a role the owner may give one user besides the Platform Owner: manage every StudioFlow template and
   setting, and override status and the phase tracker when a PIC is away, without asking the Platform Owner.
3. Collaboration between designers (tagging a colleague) is a rare nice-to-have: kept as is, not extended.
4. The Platform Owner does not read staff members' private books. The Manager answers a client round with the buttons in
   the phase strip, not through the book.

## Locked decisions

- Manager profile = every Designer permission except the PIC position, plus `project.manage`, `project.override`,
  `phase.override` (hard reset of a phase's history; owner, 2026-10-24) and `settings.manage`. No Ideas, no PIC
  position (a Manager who also works as a PIC is given the Designer or Drafter profile as well).
- One override rule, unchanged and now documented in one place: `hasProjectAssignmentOverride` needs `project.manage` and
  `project.override` together (the role editor grants them as a pair). Making `project.override` enough on its own was
  tried and dropped: it would widen a half-configured role, and the existing access tests protect against that.
- The matrix lives in `docs/apps/studioflow/ACCESS-MATRIX.md`, generated by `access-matrix.test.ts`.
- Existing databases: migration `20261024200000_studioflow_manager_profile` replaces only the StudioFlow grants of the
  canonical `studio-manager` role. It applies when the branch is merged and migrated; a Manager role customised by hand
  in the role editor is overwritten for StudioFlow permissions only.

## Not changed

Book privacy, the PIC rules per phase seat, Designer and Drafter profiles, other apps' permissions, who is a book reader.
